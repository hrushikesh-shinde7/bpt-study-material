Community Physiotherapy, in syllabus order

Every part of the MUHS Community Physiotherapy syllabus in its own order, so you can follow your classes. Each page is built from the 20 papers from Summer 2016 to Summer 2026. 20 of 27 pages are ready; the rest are being written in order of how often they are asked.

  1. Follow your classes. Open the part your college is teaching.
  2. Read a page once, slowly. Open every "Why?" button.
  3. Close it and recall. Write the keywords and draw the diagrams on paper from memory, then compare.
  4. Test yourself with the questions at the end of the page. Answer before you open the answer.
  5. Come back on day 1, day 7 and day 21, and tick the boxes at the top of the page.
What the real paper looks like (20 papers, 2016–2026)

80 marks in 3 hours. No MCQs, even though the syllabus printed a scheme with them.

  • Short answers: any 5 of 6 at 3 marks, and any 5 of 6 at 7 marks.
  • Long answers: two questions, each any 1 of 2 at 15 marks. Most options are a person in the community (an office worker with neck pain, a new mother, an older person who falls, an obese teenager, an amputee in a village), and many ask for a functional diagnosis with goals.
