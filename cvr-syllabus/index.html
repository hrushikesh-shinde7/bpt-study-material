  2. Read a page once, slowly. Open every "Why?" button.
  3. Close it and recall. Write the keywords and draw the diagrams on paper from memory, then compare.

  5. Come back on day 1, day 7 and day 21, and tick the boxes at the top of the page. Testing yourself again beats re-reading.
What the real paper looks like (20 papers, 2016–2026)

80 marks in 3 hours. No MCQs, even though the syllabus printed a scheme with them.

  • Short answers: any 5 of 6 at 3 marks, and any 5 of 6 at 7 marks.
  • Long answers: two questions, each any 1 of 2 at 15 marks. Every option is a patient case, and almost all start with ICF.

Under each item, the grey line says how often it was asked from Summer 2016 to Summer 2026. Since 2023 an ICU question has appeared in every paper, so give Chapter 9 the same time as the Tier 1 conditions in Chapter 6.
