Safe lifting and handling

  • Assess task, person, environment, equipment → plan and communicate; patient does as much as possible.
  • Equipment instead of lifting: slide sheets, transfer boards, stand-aids, hoists for dependent patients.
  • Wide base, bend knees, load close, back upright, turn with the feet.
  • Never the drag lift (under the arms): injures the hemiplegic shoulder and the carer's back.
  • Current: technique training alone did not prevent back pain (Cochrane 2011): use equipment.
  • Transfers: independent (pivot, sliding board) → one helper (pivot to stronger side, block weak knee) → two helpers, stand-aid → hoist.

Parts: frame; seat, back, cushion; rear wheels, push rims; castors; brakes; armrests; footrests or legrests; push handles; anti-tip bars; lap belt.

Prescription (LAQ S26)

WHO 8 steps: referral → assessment → prescription → funding and ordering → product preparation → fitting → user training → follow-up and repairs.

Assess: stable or progressive, trunk and head control, arm strength, deformity (pelvic obliquity, scoliosis), skin, breathing, growth; home, school, rough rural roads, carer, budget, local repair.

Measurements (seated on the cushion)

Seat widthHip width + 5 cm
Seat depthButtock to back of knee − 5 cm (too deep: slumps)
FootplateThighs level; 5 cm off the ground
Back heightBelow scapulae (active); higher + head support for poor trunk
ArmrestElbow height + 2.5 cm (elbow 90°)

Seating: solid seat and back, laterals, hip guides, pommel, headrest, lap belt; pressure cushion.

Modifications by condition

  • Hemiplegia: hemi-height seat, arm trough or lap tray, brake extension on the weak side.
  • Paraplegia: light rigid frame, low back, good cushion, removable armrests for board transfers.
  • C5–C6: rim projections or coated rims, gloves, higher back; power for distance. C4 and above: power with chin or breath control and tilt.
  • CP: custom seating (pelvic belt, pommel, laterals, headrest), tilt.
  • DMD: manual for distance early → power with tilt, recline, elevating legrests, spinal supports. MND: tilt, head support, plan power early.

Pressure care and skills

Cushion (foam, gel, air) not bottomed out; weight shifts every 15–30 min, held ≥ 1 min (push-up, forward or side lean, tilt); skin checks twice daily with a mirror. Teach: brakes, long strokes, turns, ramps, kerbs (back-wheel balance), transfers, getting back in after a fall, maintenance.

Adaptive devices and home

Aids: stick, tripod, quadripod, crutches, walker, rollator; handgrip at wrist crease, elbow 20–30°; axillary top 5 cm below armpit; stick opposite the weak leg. ADL: built-up cutlery, button hook, reacher, raised toilet seat, grab rails, shower chair. Ramp: no steeper than 1 in 12, ≥ 1200 mm wide, rails both sides at 760–900 mm (India 2021). ADIP scheme: free or subsidised aids via ALIMCO with disability certificate ≥ 40%.

Exam plan (prescription in a long case)

Assess person and setting → type with reasons → measurements, seating, cushion → fit, train user and family → review with growth or progression.
