Asked: long cases W24 (Friedreich's ataxia, 20-year-old woman: ICF, goals, PT), W22 (cerebellar stroke, Berg 38: ICF, PT), W18 (Friedreich's picture: structural impairments, tests, PT). 7 marks: PT of cerebellar ataxia (S16, W16), rationale of balance training (W19). 3 marks: rebound test (W17), assessment of sensory ataxia (S19).

Definition, types, causes

Ataxia: errors in direction, range, timing and force of movement, not due to weakness. Cerebellar: eyes open and closed, nystagmus, dysarthria, intention tremor; hemisphere → same-side limbs, vermis → trunk and gait. Sensory (posterior columns, nerves): worse eyes closed, Romberg positive, stamping gait. Vestibular: vertigo, limbs normal. Causes: posterior stroke, Friedreich's, MS, alcohol, phenytoin, tumour, B12 and vitamin E lack, ataxic CP.

Features, rebound (3m W17), sensory ataxia (3m S19)

Failed error correction → dysmetria, decomposition, intention tremor; dysdiadochokinesia, hypotonia; titubation, wide-based staggering gait, no tandem walk; scanning speech. Slower motor learning → more repetitions.

Rebound test: patient flexes elbow against resistance; examiner suddenly lets go, guarding the face; cerebellar: arm flies to the face (Holmes' rebound). Sensory ataxia: Romberg; position and vibration (128 Hz) lost from the toes; finger-nose, heel-shin worse eyes closed; pseudoathetosis; stamping, watches feet.

Friedreich's (W18, W24): autosomal recessive (GAA, frataxin), before 25; gait and limb ataxia, dysarthria, lost ankle jerks + extensor plantars, lost position sense, pes cavus, kyphoscoliosis, cardiomyopathy (main death cause), diabetes; wheelchair in 10–20 years. W18 structural (2m): spinocerebellar, posterior column, corticospinal degeneration; spine and foot deformity.

Assessment (W18)

Finger-nose, heel-shin, rapid alternating, rebound, circles, eyes open and closed; Romberg; tandem walk; Berg (below 45 = fall risk), TUG, 10 m; SARA (8 items, 0–40); Nine-Hole Peg; spine, feet.

ICF (W24: 20, Friedreich's 2 years, tremor, falls, poor fine motor)

  • Structure/function: gait and limb ataxia, intention tremor; lost position sense (vision helps only partly); poor balance; dysarthria; possible scoliosis, cardiomyopathy.
  • Activity: repeated falls, stairs, writing, buttoning, eating. Participation: college, travel, outings, marriage plans.
  • Environment: family, devices (facilitators); stairs, crowded transport, stigma (barriers). Personal: young, educated, anxious.

Goals: short-term (4–6 weeks): knows fall triggers, aid outdoors; better Berg and SARA; writes with adapted pen; cycles 20 min. Long-term (3–6 months): fewer falls; walks safely at college with an aid; independent self-care and study.

W22 (65, right cerebellar stroke, diabetic, Berg 38): right limb and truncal ataxia, high fall risk, neuropathy adds sensory loss → balance over 3 h a week, right-limb coordination, stick or rollator, sugar check.

PT (7m S16, W16; rationale 7m W19)

  • Intensive coordinative training daily for at least 4 weeks, then home programme (Ilg 2009: gains lasted a year).
  • Proximal stability: prone on elbows → four-point → kneeling → half-kneeling → standing; approximation; rhythmic stabilisation.
  • Balance: reducing base, reaching, stepping, perturbation; over 3 h a week for falls. Gait: wide base then narrow, lines, obstacles, treadmill, rollator or weighted walker.
  • Arm: slow movements to targets with feedback, elbows on table, weighted cutlery and pens. Sensory part: Frenkel's, vision first.
  • Fitness: stationary or recumbent cycling towards 150 min a week. Friedreich's: posture, foot orthoses, seating.

Rationale (W19): the cerebellum adapts through practice and error; repeated balance challenge teaches remaining circuits to reduce sway, strengthens postural muscles, builds confidence, cuts falls.

Precautions

Gait belt, bars; Friedreich's: breathlessness, palpitations, sugar; cerebellar stroke: new headache, vomiting, drowsiness; fatigue worsens ataxia; no alcohol or sedatives.

Exam plan

Restate type, cause, falls → ICF, cerebellar vs sensory signs → tests, SARA → intensive coordination, stability, gait, arm, Frenkel's, fitness. Draw: ICF; wide-based gait. Underline: dysmetria, intention tremor, Romberg, proximal stability.
