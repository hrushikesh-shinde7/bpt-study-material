Short answers: 3 and 7 marks

3 marks (half a page): one-line definition → 3–5 points, small table or labelled sketch → one line of PT relevance. Example (dysreflexia): sudden dangerous BP rise in SCI at or above T6 from a stimulus below the lesion; pounding headache, flushing and sweating above, slow pulse, blocked nose; sit up, legs down, loosen clothing, check bladder then bowel, monitor BP; commonest cause a full bladder.

7 marks (1.5–2 pages): definition (2 lines) → classification, cause or "why" (flowchart or table) → 6–8 headed points: assessment, aims, techniques with doses, by stage or problem → labelled diagram → precautions → one closing line (outcome measure or evidence).

The 15-mark case

  1. Read the split (5 + 7 + 3, 7 + 8) and give space in proportion.
  2. Underline clues: age, job, earning role, family, home (stairs, rural), time since onset, main complaint, what the patient wants.
  3. Draw the ICF model, then the ICF table with a clinical reasoning column and a one-line functional diagnosis (ICF).
  4. Problem list by priority → SMART goals, short and long term, with time frames.
  5. Management by phase, then by impairment: technique, dose, reason; precautions; education, home programme.
  6. Outcome measures and discharge criteria in 1–2 lines.

UMN cases (stroke, SCI, MS, CP, brain injury): tone, motor control, task practice, balance, gait, cognition, contracture prevention. LMN cases (GBS, nerve injury, polio, neuropathy): protect denervated muscle and numb skin, strengthen by grade, splints, breathing in GBS, re-education with reinnervation.

Diagrams and common mistakes

Draw: ICF model (every case); motor pathway with UMN and LMN; circle of Willis; incomplete cord syndromes; dysreflexia flowchart; bladder types; brachial plexus; nerve injury classification; strength-duration curve; gait cycle; iliotibial band in polio; spina bifida types; neuromuscular junction. Simple, labelled, large; never instead of text.

Mistakes: writing all you know instead of the parts asked; goals without time frames; treatment without doses; no precautions; ignoring job, family, home; old answers (passive stretching alone, bed rest, no exercise in MS or myasthenia).
