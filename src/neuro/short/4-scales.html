Asked: 3 marks only: Glasgow Coma Scale (W16 with two conditions, S19, S20, S26 interpretation); Modified Ashworth Scale (S19, W21); ASIA scale (S19); Hoehn and Yahr (W20). Expect independence measures as 7 marks; a named measure strengthens every long case.

GCS (3m W16, S19, S20, S26)

Eyes (E 1–4)4 spontaneous; 3 to sound; 2 to pressure; 1 none
Verbal (V 1–5)5 oriented; 4 confused; 3 words; 2 sounds; 1 none
Motor (M 1–6)6 obeys; 5 localises; 4 normal flexion; 3 abnormal flexion; 2 extension; 1 none

Total 3–15: severe 3–8, moderate 9–12, mild 13–15. Report parts ("E2 V2 M4 = 8"); motor score predicts outcome best. Used in head injury, stroke, meningitis, overdose. See brain injury.

MAS (3m S19, W21), ASIA (S19), Hoehn and Yahr (W20)

MAS: 0 no increase; 1 catch and release or minimal resistance at end of range; 1+ catch then minimal resistance through less than half the remaining range; 2 more marked through most of range, part moves easily; 3 considerable, passive movement difficult; 4 rigid. Patient relaxed; full range in about 1 s; grade the first movement.

ASIA (AIS): A complete, no function in S4–S5; B sensory incomplete (sensation, no motor below level, including S4–S5); C motor incomplete, less than half key muscles below level grade 3; D half or more grade 3 or more; E normal in someone who had deficits.

Hoehn and Yahr: 1 one side; 2 both sides, balance normal; 3 both sides, some postural instability, independent; 4 severe disability, still walks or stands unaided; 5 wheelchair or bed unless helped. Modified adds 1.5 (one side + trunk), 2.5 (recovers on pull test).

Independence and quality of life

Barthel: 10 items (feeding, bathing, grooming, dressing, bowels, bladder, toilet use, transfers, level mobility, stairs); steps of 5; 0–100. Shah: 0–20 total, 21–60 severe, 61–90 moderate, 91–99 slight dependence; 100 independent (not free of disability). Ceiling effect. FIM: 18 items (13 motor, 5 cognitive), each 1–7, 18–126; includes cognition, needs training. Modified Rankin (stroke): 0 no symptoms; 1 no significant disability; 2 slight, looks after own affairs; 3 moderate, needs some help, walks unaided; 4 cannot walk or attend to bodily needs without help; 5 bedridden, incontinent, constant care; 6 dead.

QoL (the person's own view; asked, not observed): SF-36 36 questions, 8 domains (physical functioning, physical role, pain, general health, vitality, social, emotional role, mental health), each 0–100; EQ-5D 5 dimensions + 0–100 scale; disease-specific: Stroke Impact Scale, PDQ-39.

Good measure: reliability (test–retest, inter-rater), validity, responsiveness, MCID, no floor or ceiling effect, feasibility. Match the ICF level; same tester and conditions on retest; put numbers in goals ("Berg 38 to 46 in 6 weeks").

By condition: stroke: Fugl-Meyer, MAS, Berg, TUG, Barthel, mRS; SCI: ISNCSCI, SCI Independence Measure; PD: UPDRS III, Hoehn and Yahr, PDQ-39; MS: EDSS; brain injury: GCS, Rancho, FIM, Glasgow Outcome Scale; CP: MAS or Tardieu, GMFCS, GMFM.
