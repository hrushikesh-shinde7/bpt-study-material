Explain domains of ICF

ICF (International Classification of Functioning, Disability and Health, WHO 2001) is a biopsychosocial framework: disability is the result of an interaction between a health condition and contextual (environmental and personal) factors. Functioning is the positive side, disability the negative side.

Component (code)Domains (chapters)Neuro example
Body functions (b)Mental; sensory and pain; voice and speech; cardiovascular, haematological, immunological, respiratory; digestive, metabolic, endocrine; genitourinary, reproductive; neuromusculoskeletal and movement-related; skinMuscle power b730, tone b735
Body structures (s)Nervous system; eye and ear; voice and speech; cardiovascular and respiratory; digestive; genitourinary; structures related to movement; skinBrain, spinal cord, nerve
Activities and participation (d)Learning and applying knowledge; general tasks; communication; mobility; self-care; domestic life; relationships; major life areas (education, work); community, social and civic lifeTransferring d420, walking d450
Environmental factors (e)Products and technology; natural and built environment; support and relationships; attitudes; services, systems and policiesWalking aid e120, family e310
Personal factorsNot classified: age, sex, education, coping, habits, motivationMotivated young teacher

Qualifiers: 0 no, 1 mild, 2 moderate, 3 severe, 4 complete problem (b730.3 = severe weakness); environmental factors take + for facilitators and – (or plain number) for barriers.

Uses: common language for the team, functional diagnosis, SMART goals at each level, capacity vs performance, documentation for disability certificates. The arrows are two-way: treat at more than one level.
