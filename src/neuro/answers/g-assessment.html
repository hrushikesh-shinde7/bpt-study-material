UMN and LMN lesions: features and differences

FeatureUMN lesionLMN lesion
SiteCortex, internal capsule, brainstem, spinal cord tractsAnterior horn cell, root, plexus, nerve
ToneSpasticity (clasp-knife)Flaccidity
ReflexesExaggerated, clonusReduced or absent
PlantarExtensor (Babinski)Flexor or absent
WastingLittle (disuse only)Marked, early
FasciculationAbsentMay be present
WeaknessGroups and patterns (pyramidal distribution), synergiesIndividual muscles in a root or nerve distribution

Examples: stroke, SCI (UMN); nerve injury, GBS, polio (LMN). MND shows both.
