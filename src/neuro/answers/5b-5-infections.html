Kernig's and Brudzinski's signs

SignMethodPositive
Kernig'sSupine; flex hip and knee to 90°, then try to straighten the kneePain and resistance in the back of the thigh stop knee extension
Brudzinski's neckSupine; flex the neck passively, chin to chestHips and knees flex involuntarily
Brudzinski's contralateral legFlex one hip and knee passivelyThe other leg flexes too

Meaning: meningeal irritation (meningitis, subarachnoid haemorrhage); stretch on inflamed roots. Low sensitivity (positive in only about 5% of adults with meningitis), so a negative sign does not rule it out.
