Horner's syndrome: features

Loss of the sympathetic supply to one side of the face and eye. Same-side features:

  • Ptosis (mild drooping of the upper lid).
  • Miosis (small pupil, most obvious in dim light; still reacts to light).
  • Anhidrosis (no sweating of the face); apparent enophthalmos.

Causes by neuron: first order (lateral medullary stroke, cervical cord injury, syringomyelia); second order (T1 root avulsion in Klumpke's palsy, Pancoast tumour); third order (carotid dissection). In plexus injury it means preganglionic avulsion: poor outlook for the hand.
