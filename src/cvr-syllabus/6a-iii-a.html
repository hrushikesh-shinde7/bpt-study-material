#Definitions

  • Valvular heart disease: a heart valve that does not open fully (stenosis) or does not close fully (regurgitation).
  • Rheumatic heart disease (RHD): permanent valve damage after rheumatic fever, which follows untreated streptococcal throat infection. Still common in India; the mitral valve is most often affected.
  • Valve replacement: a damaged valve replaced by a mechanical valve (lifelong warfarin) or a tissue valve, by open surgery through a sternotomy or through a catheter (TAVI).

Valve disease is one kind of acquired heart disease. It often ends in heart failure, so read it with 6(a)(v), which covers heart failure, cardiomyopathy and cardiac devices.
