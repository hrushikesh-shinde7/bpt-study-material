#Ulcers in this syllabus item

An ulcer is an open break in the skin that is slow to heal. Syllabus 6(h) covers two groups:

  • Diabetic (neuropathic) foot ulcers: on this page.
  • Vascular ulcers: venous ulcers, whose full care is with varicose veins in 6(a)(vii), and arterial (ischaemic) ulcers, with peripheral arterial disease in 6(a)(viii).

Pressure sores (W19) and wound healing (S19, S25) are also on this page, because they apply to every ulcer. Amputation, the other half of 6(h), has its own page next.
