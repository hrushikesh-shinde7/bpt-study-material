#Home exercise programmes

A home programme turns treatment into daily habits. A good one has the same five parts for every condition:

  • What: a few exercises the patient can do alone (breathing, airway clearance, walking, strengthening), written down, with pictures if possible.
  • How much: frequency, intensity, time and type, with a simple self-check such as the talk test, the Borg score or SpO₂.
  • Safety: warning signs, when to stop and whom to call.
  • Context: fitted to the home and the job, which are the ICF contextual factors: stairs with no lift, Indian toilets, a hot kitchen, long standing or sitting at work, travel, and who at home can help.
  • Review: a diary or checklist, and a follow-up date.

Each condition's programme is at the end of its notes:

  • Heart and vessels: hypertension, IHD and CABG, heart failure, venous disease and foot care, peripheral arterial disease.
  • Lungs: COPD, asthma, ILD, TB, bronchiectasis, pleural disease.
  • Surgery and other conditions: lung surgery, abdominal surgery, mastectomy and arm care, burns, amputation and stump care, diabetes, obesity.
  • Long-term programmes: cardiac rehabilitation Phases III and IV, pulmonary rehabilitation.
