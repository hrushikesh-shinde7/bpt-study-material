Asked: not a theory question. The practical is 80 marks: one long case (50) from rehabilitation, women's health, geriatrics, industrial health or health promotion; project synopsis (25); journal (5). Internal assessment adds 20.

Marking scheme

Long case 50Assessment, ICF diagnosis, goals, plan, demonstration, viva
Synopsis 25Defend the internship study: introduction, aims, methods, review of literature
Journal 5Five cases (one per area) + at least one visit: industry, geriatric home or community assessment

Terminal: two long cases × 40. Preliminary: one long case 50 + synopsis 30.

Community long-case proforma

Subjective: age, sex, education, marital status, family (joint or nuclear, who helps), income, socioeconomic status; complaints in the person's words; history (obstetric and menstrual in women, falls in the elderly); occupation (hours, shifts, postures, loads, tools, breaks, environment; job and task analysis); home (floor level, stairs, Indian or western toilet, floor, lighting, distance to services, transport); lifestyle (activity, diet, sleep, tobacco, stress); the person's goals; knowledge of schemes.

Objective: vitals, height, weight, BMI, waist; posture, range, strength, flexibility, special tests; ADL and IADL, gait, balance (TUG, Berg), endurance (6-minute walk), fitness tests. Area-specific: PERFECT and diastasis recti (women); MMSE, GDS, frailty (elderly); REBA, RULA, FCE items (workers); disability percentage and aids (rehabilitation). Outcome measures to repeat: NPRS, NDI or Oswestry, ICIQ, FES-I, Barthel.

Then: ICF table, one-line functional diagnosis → problem list → short- and long-term goals → plan, home programme → community and workplace changes → schemes and referrals.

Add by area

IndustrialJob and task analysis, ergonomic score, workstation drawing, hazards, work conditioning and hardening, return to work (disability management)
Women's healthObstetric history, stage of pregnancy or postpartum, pelvic floor and diastasis, feeding and lifting posture, safe exercise, warning signs
GeriatricsComprehensive scales, falls risk, home hazards, carer, institution or home
Health promotionFitness tests, BMI and body composition, risk factors, FITT with heart-rate target, lifestyle plan
RehabilitationDisability type and percentage, aids, transport, accessibility, CBR plan, schemes, concessions, vocational plans

Presenting the case (5 minutes)

  1. One sentence: "Mrs A, 38, homemaker with two children, leaks urine on coughing and lifting for 2 years."
  2. Key findings in ICF order (draw the ICF if asked) → one-line functional diagnosis.
  3. Two or three priority problems with short- and long-term goals.
  4. Plan: treatment with doses, home programme, ergonomic or home changes, health promotion, schemes, referrals.
  5. Demonstrate one test and one treatment: PERFECT and pelvic floor teaching; REBA and a workstation exercise; TUG and a balance exercise.

Synopsis (25) and journal (5)

Synopsis: two copies, consent form, proforma. Explain question (FINER, PICO), design, sample size, criteria, outcome measures, statistics, ethics; admit limitations (synopsis).

Journal: five cases with assessment, ICF diagnosis, goals, intervention, outcome. Visit record: date, place, purpose, people met, observations (tasks and hazards, ergonomic problems, facilities for elders, accessibility), PT recommendations, what you learnt. Neat, signed, up to date; any entry may be asked.

What examiners watch

Introduction, consent, explanation, privacy and draping (women's health); safety: BP and warning signs, guard the elderly, no heavy testing in pregnancy or osteoporosis; practical, affordable advice for the person's home, job, income; correct, current facts on schemes and laws; honest answers ("I would refer to the doctor"), not guesses.

Last two weeks

Practise the proforma on classmates until it takes under 20 minutes; rehearse one case from each of the five areas aloud in ICF order; rehearse the synopsis and common examiner questions; check the journal and visit report.
