Asked: long cases: 38-year-old teacher found at a village camp 3 weeks after below-knee amputation, functional diagnosis + CBR (S17); 37-year-old truck driver, difficulties + concessions (W18); 32-year-old truck driver, disability evaluation principles + management (W19). 7 marks: psychosocial issues (W17); prosthetic check-out (S18).

Below-knee prosthesis

Socket: PTB or total surface bearing, soft liner. Suspension: supracondylar cuff or wedge, sleeve, pin-lock liner, suction; thigh corset if very short. Shank: exoskeletal or endoskeletal (pylon). Foot: SACH, single-axis, dynamic; Jaipur foot (Sethi, BMVSS, free): cheap, waterproof, allows squatting, cross-legged sitting, barefoot walking.

Tolerant: patellar tendon, medial tibial flare, fibular shaft, pretibial muscles, popliteal (lightly). Relieved: tibial crest and tubercle, fibular head, distal tibia and fibula, hamstring tendons, patella.

Pre-prosthetic and prosthetic training

Stump: wound, skin check, desensitisation; figure-of-eight bandage (tighter distally) or shrinker, day and night → cone. Prevent knee and hip flexion contracture: no pillow under the knee, stump board, prone 20–30 min a few times a day. Strengthen knee, hip extensors and abductors, trunk, arms; one-leg balance; transfers, crutches. With prosthesis: donning, wearing time built up, skin check, weight shift, gait, stairs, slopes, squatting.

Check-out (7m S18)

Before donningAs prescribed; socket smooth; suspension, foot right; stump skin intact
StandingEqual weight; pelvis level; knee slight flexion; foot flat, slight toe-out; total contact; patellar bar level; pylon vertical
SittingKnee ~90° without posterior wall pinching; stays on
WalkingNo deviations, no pistoning, no pain; equal steps; stairs
After removalRedness only on tolerant areas, fading in 10–20 min; no blisters

Deviations: excess knee flexion at heel strike (foot dorsiflexed or back, hard heel); knee stays straight (foot plantarflexed or forward, soft heel); lateral thrust = foot inset; medial thrust = outset; early drop-off (short keel); pistoning (loose socket: add socks).

Psychosocial issues (7m W17)

Grief (shock → acceptance); depression, anxiety; body image; phantom pain; lost role and income (drivers, farmers); dependence, family and marital strain; stigma, beliefs; prosthesis cost. Help: counselling, peer support from an amputee; early fitting; phantom pain: explanation, desensitisation, mirror therapy, TENS; family; vocational rehab, schemes, sport.

Disability evaluation (W19), difficulties and concessions (W18)

Medical board after maximum improvement; measured function. Below-knee (BK) up to 8 cm 70%, lower third 60%, through-knee 75%, above-knee 80–85%, Syme's 50%; +5% stump unfit, up to +10% complications; two limbs a + b(90 − a) ÷ 90, +10%, max 100%; 40% = benchmark (disability).

Difficulties: distance, roads, stairs, Indian toilet, energy cost; right leg: driving needs adapted controls; income; repairs. Concessions: certificate, UDID; free prosthesis (ADIP, BMVSS, DDRC); rail and bus; pension, tax, loans; 4% jobs, RPWD job protection; accident claims tribunal, employees' compensation, ESI.

ICF and CBR (S17: 38, teacher, BK 3 weeks, remote village)

  • Structure/function: healing swollen stump, weak deconditioned muscles. Activity: walking, Indian toilet, stairs.
  • Participation: teaching, village life. Environment: remote, no prosthetic centre. Personal: 38, teacher.

CBR: family and CBR worker taught bandaging, prone lying, exercises; worker weekly, PT monthly; certificate at camp → DDRC, Jaipur foot or ADIP camp; carpenter: parallel bars, stump board, commode stool; panchayat: ramp, rails; early return to teaching (chair, ground floor); peer support, self-help group; follow-up (TUG) (CBR).
