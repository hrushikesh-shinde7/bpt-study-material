Definitions and components

Physical activity: any movement by skeletal muscles that uses energy. Exercise: planned, structured, repeated, to improve or keep fitness. Physical fitness: attributes that relate to the ability to perform physical activity (Caspersen 1985). Health-related (5): cardiorespiratory endurance, strength, muscular endurance, flexibility, body composition. Skill-related (6): agility, balance, coordination, speed, power, reaction time.

Fitness tests (3m S26) and field tests (7m S16)

Tests: cardiorespiratory: YMCA step, Rockport walk, Cooper run, 6MWT; strength: 1RM, grip; endurance: push-up, curl-up; flexibility: sit-and-reach, back scratch; body composition: BMI, waist, skinfolds; agility: Illinois, T-test; balance: stork, Y-balance; coordination: wall toss; speed: 30–50 m sprint; power: jumps; reaction: ruler-drop.

  • Field test: away from the laboratory, no expensive kit (NSCA); must be valid and reliable.
  • Beep test: 20 m shuttles to beeps, 8.5 km/h, +0.5 each minute; last stage → VO₂max. Yo-Yo IR: 2 × 20 m, 10 s jog; level 1 from 10 km/h; distance. Cooper 12 min: r 0.897 with treadmill.
  • RAST: 6 × 35 m, 10 s rest; power = mass × distance² ÷ time³. Jumps: Sargent (reach difference), broad jump.
  • T-test: 10 yd forward, shuffle 5 left, 10 right, 5 back, backpedal (reliability 0.98). Illinois excellent (16–19 y): < 15.2 s boys, < 17.0 s girls. These test change of direction, not reactive agility.
  • Order (NSCA): non-fatiguing (height, weight, flexibility, skinfold, vertical jump) → agility → strength, power → sprint → muscular endurance → anaerobic → aerobic last. Limits: weather, motivation; values predicted.

Energy systems (7m W16)

  • ATP is the only direct fuel; stored for seconds. Phosphagen: creatine phosphate + ADP → ATP; fastest, smallest. Fast glycolysis: glucose → lactate, net 2 ATP. Oxidative: mitochondria, ~30–32 ATP/glucose; slowest, largest.
  • NSCA: 0–6 s phosphagen; 6–30 s + glycolysis; 30 s–2 min glycolysis; 2–3 min glycolysis + oxidative; > 3 min oxidative.
  • Lactate threshold 50–60% VO₂max untrained, 70–80% trained. EPOC after exercise.
  • Work : rest: phosphagen 1:12–1:20; glycolysis 1:3–1:5; oxidative 1:1–1:3 or continuous.

Principles (3m S19; 7m S16) and FITT-VP, ACSM (3m W20)

Overload; progression (duration → frequency → intensity, ~10% a week); specificity; reversibility ("use it or lose it"); individuality; recovery (else overtraining).

Prescription steps (7m W25) and phases (7m S16)

Screen (history, risk factors, drugs, clearance) → test, set goals → write FITT-VP → teach warm-up, cool-down, talk test, RPE, warning signs → retest every 4–6 weeks. Phases: warm-up 5–10 min → conditioning 20–60 min → cool-down 5–10 min (no pooling, dizziness) → stretch ≥ 10 min. Stages: initial → improvement → maintenance.

Circuit training (7m S25)

Stations one after another with little rest, muscle groups alternating, aerobic stations added; repeated. Morgan and Adamson, Leeds, 1953. Method: 10–15 stations; 30–40 s or 12–15 reps at 40–60% 1RM; 15–30 s to move on; 1–3 circuits (~30 min). Types: weight, aerobic, high-intensity (12 × 30 s, 10 s change), circuit class therapy, sport-specific. Effect: VO₂max +5% men, +8% women; +11% with HR at 70–80% max. Stroke circuit class: 6MWT +61 m (Cochrane 2017). Precautions: technique; no breath-holding; RPE.

Sedentary lifestyle (7m W18)

Sedentary behaviour: awake, ≤ 1.5 METs, sitting, reclining or lying. Physical inactivity: not meeting guidelines (SBRN 2017); separate risks. 31% of adults worldwide, India ~49% (women 57%) insufficiently active (2022); ICMR-INDIAB 54.4% inactive. 20–30% higher death risk (WHO). High sitting: death HR 1.24; diabetes 1.91; CVD death 1.18; cancer death 1.17; depression RR 1.25. Also obesity, colon and breast cancer, sarcopenia, osteoporosis, back pain. Risk rises above 6–8 h/day sitting; 60–75 min/day moderate activity offsets it.

Prescribe: WHO 150–300 min + strength 2 days; sit less; 2-min walk every 20 min (glucose −24–30%); desk workers 2 h → 4 h/day standing and light walking; sit-stand desk; stairs, walking meetings, reminders.

Bed rest deconditioning (7m S16; 3m W16)

Heart: ↑ resting HR, ↓ stroke volume, ↓ VO₂max, postural hypotension, DVT. Lungs: basal collapse, pneumonia. Muscle: antigravity wasting, ~1–1.5% strength a day. Bone, joints: bone loss, urine calcium, contractures. Skin: sores. Metabolic: insulin resistance. Gut, kidney: constipation, stones. Mind: confusion, low mood. Prevent: sit out, walk early; breathing; ankle pumps; resisted exercise; turning; early weight-bearing.

Exercise in hypertension (7m W25)

India: 35.5% of adults (ICMR-INDIAB 2023). During: systolic rises, diastolic level. Acute: BP lower up to 22 h (post-exercise hypotension). Training: 5–8 mmHg fall in hypertension. Edwards 2023: aerobic 4.5/2.5, resistance 4.6/3.0, combined 6.0/2.5, isometric 8.2/4.0 (best). How: noradrenaline −29%, renin −20%, resistance −7%; endothelium; stiffness; weight, insulin. ACSM: aerobic 5–7 days, 40–59% HRR, ≥ 30 min; resistance 2–3 days, 60–70% 1RM, 2–4 × 8–12. Classic "no weights" is outdated: no breath-holding.

Precautions: diabetes (3m S18), hypertension

  • Diabetes (ADA 2016): ≥ 150 min/week, ≥ 3 days, ≤ 2 days in a row off; resistance 2–3 days. Glucose before and after if on insulin or sulphonylureas; fast sugar; not the exercising limb; lows 6–15 h later, risk to 48 h; no exercise with high glucose + ketones.
  • Feet: check, footwear, cycling or swimming if ulcer. Retinopathy: no heavy lifting, jarring, head-down, breath-holding. Autonomic neuropathy: RPE, slow position change.
  • Hypertension: no start if resting BP > 200/110; stop test > 250/115; long warm-up, cool-down (vasodilators, alpha-blockers); RPE with beta-blockers; diuretics: dehydration, low potassium.
