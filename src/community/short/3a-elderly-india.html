Senior citizens in India

Senior citizen: aged 60 or over (2007 Act). 104 million (8.6%) in Census 2011 → ~227 million (14.9%) by 2036; more elderly than under-15s by ~2046. Mostly rural; feminisation of ageing; oldest old (80+) growing fastest; most without pension.

Social issues: joint family break-up, elders alone; financial dependence; multiple diseases, poor access; loneliness, lost role; elder abuse; unsafe housing (stairs, Indian toilets).

Policies and schemes (7m S18; 3m W18)

Maintenance and Welfare Act 2007Children must maintain parents; tribunals (up to ₹10,000 a month); old age homes; abandonment punishable
NPHCE 2010Geriatric wards at district hospitals; rehab and PT at CHCs; home care via PHCs
PM-JAY 70+ (2024)₹5 lakh a year cover for all aged 70+ (Vay Vandana card)
Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana 2017Free aids (sticks, walkers, hearing aids, spectacles, dentures) at camps

Also: IGNOAPS pension below poverty line; Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (elderly and palliative care); tax and travel benefits.

NGOs (3m W19)

HelpAge India, Dignity Foundation, Agewell. Roles: mobile units and camps (eye, hearing, PT); old age homes, day care; helplines, abuse protection; livelihood and pension help; sponsoring destitute elders; advocacy; caregiver training; linking to schemes.

Home vs institution vs hospital (S18, S20, W17)

Home: familiar, independence, dignity, cheaper; but unsafe stairs and toilet, loneliness, no emergency help. Institution: supervision, safety, meals, medicines, peers; but lost independence and privacy, separation, cost, depression. Hospital: acute illness only; deconditioning, delirium. Decide by: wishes, stability, ADL, cognition, falls risk, home, carer, money. Aim: home with support where safe.

ICF (S18: 65, alone, 4th floor chawl, left Girdlestone)

  • Structure/function: femoral head and neck removed: short leg, weak unstable hip, pain. Activity: walking, stairs, toileting.
  • Participation: living alone, shopping. Environment: 4th floor, no lift, shared toilet, alone. Personal: 65, independent.

Long-term goals (3–6 months): walks indoors and out with stick or walker and shoe raise; stairs with rail; independent self-care; no falls. Management: strengthen hip abductors, extensors, quads; walker → stick in right hand; stairs up with the good leg, down with the operated; balance; raised toilet seat, grab bars, phone in reach. Answer (4m): short stay with family or care facility during rehab, then home once stairs and toilet are safe; the choice is hers. S20: home with trained helper and home PT unless unstable.
