Comprehensive geriatric assessment: multidimensional team assessment of medical, functional, psychological and social needs. History: drugs (number, sedatives, BP), falls in last year, continence, nutrition, goals. Physical: BP lying and standing, vision, hearing, feet, grip, sensation.

Fitness assessment (7m S25): Senior Fitness Test

30-s chair stand (leg strength); 30-s arm curl (2 kg women, 3.6 kg men); 6-min walk or 2-min step; chair sit-and-reach; back scratch; 8-foot up-and-go (agility); height, weight, BMI. Add grip, single-leg stance, gait speed. Screen first; stop for chest pain, dizziness, unusual breathlessness.

Principles of geriatric rehab (7m W18, W19)

Comprehensive assessment; treat the person, not the age; function is the goal, set with them; teamwork; mobilise early; start low, go slow, but progress; prevent the next problem (falls, sores, contractures); environment and aids; comorbidity and drugs; simple instructions, carers; dignity and choice; continuity with community groups. PT role: exercise groups, screening, treatment, falls, home visits, carer training, palliative care.

Immobility in the elderly (3m S23)

Rapid loss of strength and walking (hospital-associated disability), contractures; postural hypotension, DVT, pneumonia; bone loss, pressure sores; incontinence, constipation, impaction; delirium, depression, fear of falling; insulin resistance, weight loss. Prevent: sit out and walk early, bed exercise, turning, toileting schedule.
