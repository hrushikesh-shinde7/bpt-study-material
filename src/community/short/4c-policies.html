Difficulties in the community (3m W17)

Physical (steps, narrow doors, toilets, no ramps); transport; attitudes (stigma, pity, overprotection, marriage); economic (unemployment, cost of aids); services (distance, no information, paperwork); education and communication; social (isolation, dependence, abuse).

Architectural barriers (7m S18)

  • Ramp: 1:12 max, 1,200 mm wide, handrails both sides (760–900 mm), landings top and bottom.
  • Doors ~900 mm, lever handles, no high thresholds; turning circle 1,500 mm.
  • Toilet: western seat at wheelchair height, grab bars, basin without pedestal, outward door, non-slip floor.
  • Lift with low buttons or ground-floor rooms; lowered counters and switches; kerb cuts, tactile paving, audible signals; large contrast signs, Braille.

Harmonised Guidelines 2021; Accessible India Campaign (2015).

Long case (S18): farmer, T12–L1, discharged

Functional diagnosis: paraplegia (ASIA), strong arms and trunk; neurogenic bladder and bowel; pressure sore risk; independent wheelchair and transfers, short walks with orthoses; rural roads limit mobility; cannot farm.

Transport: wheelchair (pneumatic tyres, cushion, wheelie skills); hand tricycle (ADIP); motorised tricycle with hand controls, licence; car with hand controls; low-floor bus, train, auto transfers; knee–ankle–foot orthoses and crutches for short distances only.

Concessions: certificate and UDID; ADIP free aids; rail 75% (second class, sleeper, first, 3-tier AC, chair car), 50% first and second AC, escort same; state bus passes; disability pension (Indira Gandhi national scheme, BPL; Sanjay Gandhi Niradhar in Maharashtra); 80U tax deduction (₹75,000; ₹1,25,000 severe); NDFDC loans (dairy, shop); 4% jobs; housing priority; panchayat ramp and toilet.
