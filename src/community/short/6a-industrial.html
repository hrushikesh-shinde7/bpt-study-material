Definitions and laws (2m of S22)

Occupational health (ILO/WHO 1950, revised 1995): highest degree of physical, mental and social well-being of workers; prevent ill health from working conditions; protect from risks; adaptation of work to people and of each person to their job.

Industrial therapy: PT (with other disciplines) applied to workers and workplaces: prevent work injury, treat disorders close to the job, return injured workers to safe, productive work. India: mostly informal workers, little protection.

Laws: Factories Act 1948 (safety, welfare, notifiable diseases); ESI Act 1948 (care and cash for sickness, maternity, injury, disablement); Employees' Compensation Act 1923; Mines Act 1952; Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020.

Industrial rehabilitation team (3m of S22)

Worker and family (active partner, reports early); employer, supervisor (modified duties, workplace change); occupational physician (diagnosis, fitness certificate, surveillance); PT (FCE, work conditioning and hardening, ergonomics); OT (hand, ADL, job simulation); ergonomist, safety officer (redesign, PPE); psychologist (fear of re-injury); vocational counsellor (retraining); case manager, insurer (ESIC), union.

Job analysis (7 + 3 of S22): description, demand analysis, task analysis (ability management). Purpose: redesign, pre-placement, FCE matching, work hardening, light duty, training, compensation.

Role of the PT in industry (7m W19)

  • Pre-placement screening; job analysis and ergonomics (REBA, RULA, NIOSH; workstation redesign).
  • Prevention, education: back school, safe lifting, micro-breaks, workstation exercises, warm-up before heavy shifts.
  • Health promotion: employee fitness, NCD risk, stress. Early care: on-site clinic, first aid.
  • Disability management: FCE, work conditioning, hardening, graded return. Hazard advice: vibration, handling, standing, PPE.
  • Records: injury data, cost–benefit. Inclusion: older and disabled workers, reasonable accommodation (RPWD Act).

Pre-placement worker care spectrum (7m S25)

Spectrum: pre-placement → ability management → (injury) → disability management → return to work. Post-offer: after a job offer, abilities tested against the essential demands of the job. Aims: safe placement; training, aid or workplace needs; baseline; fewer injuries and costs.

Components: job demand analysis; health questionnaire and exam; musculoskeletal screen; job-matched tests (lifting, carrying, push-pull, positional tolerance, grip, aerobic). Result: fit; fit with adjustments; temporarily unfit; report in functional terms. Rules: job-related, standardised, same for all; no unfair exclusion (RPWD Act 2016); confidential.
