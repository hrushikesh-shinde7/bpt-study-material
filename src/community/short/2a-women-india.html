Legal rights and benefits (W19, 8 marks)

Constitution: Art. 14 equality; Art. 15 no sex discrimination; Art. 39 equal pay; Art. 42 maternity relief; one-third panchayat seats (73rd Amendment 1992); Lok Sabha too (2023 Act).

Acts: Maternity Benefit 1961 (2017): 26 weeks paid leave, nursing breaks, crèche; Medical Termination of Pregnancy 1971 (2021): to 20 weeks, 24 special cases; PCPNDT 1994: no fetal sex determination; Dowry Prohibition 1961; Domestic Violence 2005: protection orders, shared home; Child Marriage 2006 (girl under 18); workplace harassment 2013: internal committees; Hindu Succession 2005: equal property; Code on Wages 2019.

Schemes: Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY) 2005 (cash for facility delivery); Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK) 2011 (free delivery, caesarean, drugs, transport); Matru Vandana (PMMVY) 2017 (₹5,000, first child; second if a girl); Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA) 2016 (free check-up on the 9th monthly); Anaemia Mukt Bharat; Ujjwala (LPG); Beti Bachao Beti Padhao; Mission Shakti (helpline 181); Swachh Bharat (toilets).

Health promotion for rural women (7m S21)

  1. Know the community: sarpanch, ASHA, ANM, anganwadi worker, self-help groups; problems; women's daily timetable.
  2. Existing platforms: anganwadi, Village Health, Sanitation and Nutrition Day, group meetings, mahila mandals, PHC.
  3. Message: iron, antenatal and postnatal exercise, pelvic floor, back care (water pots), bones, walking, clean fuel, toilets, screening.
  4. Methods: local language, pictures, demonstration and practice, songs, short sessions, women educators, home visits.
  5. Involve men, mothers-in-law, elders; link to schemes; train local women; evaluate (knowledge before and after, behaviour change).

Primigravidae in a village (LAQ S22, 5 + 10)

Approach (5): permission (PHC officer, panchayat, ASHA, ANM); list primigravidae from registers; sessions at anganwadi, PMSMA clinic on the 9th; invite husbands, mothers-in-law; flip charts, demonstrations.

Management (10): 1 posture, back care (lifting pots, side-lying rest); 2 exercise: walking 30 min most days, pelvic tilts, cat–camel, ankle exercises, warning signs; 3 pelvic floor; 4 labour: breathing, positions, JSY, JSSK, ambulance 108; 5 after birth: pelvic floor, abdominals, feeding posture, spacing. Throughout: iron, visits, danger signs. ASHAs repeat at home; refer pain, leakage, high BP, bleeding; evaluate knowledge, institutional delivery.
