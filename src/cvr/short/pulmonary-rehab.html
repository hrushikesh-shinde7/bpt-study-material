Asked: the long-term part (5–8 marks) of every COPD, bronchiectasis, ILD and post-TB case: write pulmonary rehab with doses. 7 marks: paediatric asthma rehab (S18), energy conservation (S19), ILD (S21), ABG, PFT, X-ray in COPD goals (W24).

Definition and aims

ATS/ERS 2013: "a comprehensive intervention based on a thorough patient assessment followed by patient-tailored therapies that include, but are not limited to, exercise training, education, and behavior change, designed to improve the physical and psychological condition of people with chronic respiratory disease and to promote the long-term adherence to health-enhancing behaviors."

Aims: less breathlessness and fatigue; ↑ capacity and function; better QoL and mood; fewer exacerbations and admissions; lasting self-management. Why it works: it treats what can change: weak muscles, poor breathing pattern, fear, inactivity, poor self-management (the breathlessness spiral).

Indications and contraindications

Indications: COPD limited by breathlessness (mMRC ≥ 2) and soon after an admission (ATS 2023 strong); ILD (strong); bronchiectasis, CF, post-TB; asthma with persistent breathlessness; pulmonary hypertension (conditional, supervised); lung cancer, before and after resection, LVRS, transplant; chest wall and neuromuscular disease; post-ICU, post-COVID. Age, smoking and low O₂ do not exclude.

Contraindications: unstable heart disease (unstable angina, recent MI, uncontrolled arrhythmia, severe AS, decompensated failure); locomotor or neurological problems preventing exercise; severe cognitive or psychiatric illness; acute exacerbation (treat first); severe pulmonary hypertension with exertional syncope, uncontrolled BP (specialist only).

Assessment

History, comorbidities (heart, diabetes, osteoporosis, anxiety, depression), drugs and inhaler technique, smoking; spirometry; mMRC; CAT, CRQ or SGRQ; HADS; 6MWT or ISWT with SpO₂ (training speed; oxygen if below ~88%); quadriceps, grip, sit-to-stand, balance; the patient's own goal ("reach my 2nd-floor flat without stopping").

Education, support, structure

Education: the disease; inhaler and spacer; written action plan; pursed lips, breathing control, positions of ease; airway clearance if sputum; energy conservation and pacing; quitting; vaccines; nutrition; travel and oxygen; panic management; advance care planning. Psychosocial and nutrition: screen and treat anxiety and depression, groups, diet, family.

Structure: 6–12 weeks, ≥ 2 supervised sessions a week + home exercise; centre, home or telerehab (ATS 2023: offer the choice); then a maintenance plan (gains fade within months).

India: few centres; home programme with stairs, chair, water-bottle weights, walking, breathing exercises and pranayama, reviewed by phone or video.
