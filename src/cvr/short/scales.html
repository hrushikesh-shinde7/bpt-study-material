Borg's own CR10 words: 0.5 extremely weak, 2 weak, 5 strong, 7 very strong, 10 extremely strong; ratings above 10 allowed. Category–ratio: word anchors (categories) spaced so 4 feels about twice 2 (ratio). Uses: before and after 6MWT, pulmonary rehab training at 3–5, stop above 7, pacing.

mMRC dyspnoea scale

0 only with strenuous exercise; 1 hurrying on the level or a slight hill; 2 slower than peers or stops at own pace on the level; 3 stops after ~100 m or a few minutes; 4 too breathless to leave the house, or when dressing. Daily-life breathlessness; GOLD "more symptoms" = mMRC ≥ 2.
