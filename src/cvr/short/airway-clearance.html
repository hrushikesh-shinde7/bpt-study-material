Asked: 7 marks: various airway clearance techniques with application, advantages, disadvantages (S19, W24); mucus removal in chronic respiratory disease (W19). 3 marks: indications of bronchial hygiene therapy (W25).

Why and classification

Normal clearance = mucociliary escalator + cough. Needed when there is too much or too thick mucus (bronchiectasis, CF, chronic bronchitis, infection), poor cilia (smoking, CF, ciliary dyskinesia, dry gas via a tube) or a weak or painful cough (neuromuscular, post-op, sedation). Aims: remove mucus, cut infections and the vicious cycle, improve ventilation and gas exchange, less cough and breathlessness, better QoL.

  • Breathing: ACBT, FET and huffing, autogenic drainage, directed or assisted cough.
  • Gravity and manual: postural drainage, percussion, vibration, shaking.
  • PEP devices: PEP mask, oscillating PEP (Flutter, Acapella), bottle PEP.
  • Mechanical: oscillation vest, IPV, cough assist, IPPB. Exercise.
  • Adjuncts: hydration, humidification, nebulised saline, mucolytics, bronchodilator first. Suction.

Choosing (BTS/ACPRC 2009)

No single best technique: choose by age, condition, ability, preference and what the patient will keep doing. Infants: modified drainage, gentle percussion, play. Older children: PEP, bubble PEP, ACBT. Wheezy or floppy airways: AD, PEP. Weak cough: assisted cough, cough assist, suction. Order: bronchodilator → mucoactive (hypertonic saline) → clearance → inhaled antibiotic or steroid. Review regularly.

Mucus removal in chronic disease (7m W19)

  • Assess: daily sputum amount and colour, ease of clearing, which lobes (X-ray, CT), breathlessness, past techniques.
  • Prepare mucus: fluids; humidified or nebulised saline; bronchodilator first in reactive airways.
  • Daily routine: one main technique (ACBT, AD or oscillating PEP), in the worst lobe's position if tolerated, 10–30 min, once or twice a day.
  • Exacerbation: 2–3 times a day; early medical review.
  • Exercise (pulmonary rehab); pelvic floor (leakage with cough: contract before huffs).
  • Review: sputum weight, cough or bronchiectasis QoL score, exacerbations.

Indications (3m W25)

Sputum over ~25–30 mL a day or hard to clear; mucus plugging with lobar or segmental collapse; weak or painful cough (neuromuscular, post-op, ventilated); chronic suppurative disease (bronchiectasis, CF) as a daily routine.
