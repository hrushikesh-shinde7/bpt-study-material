Practical exam: 80 marks + 20 internal assessment. Long case 45, short case 2 × 10, spots 5 × 2, journal 5. (College terminal and prelim: spots 5 × 3 = 15, journal no marks.)

Marking scheme

Long case 45(a) subjective and physical examination 10; (b) evaluation and PT diagnosis (ICF) 10; (c) plan of care and goals 10; (d) demonstrate one test and one treatment on the patient 15. Cognitive, psychomotor and affective domains
Short case 20Two physiotherapy skills, 10 each
Spots 10Chest or cardiac X-ray, ABG, PFT, ECG, devices: 5 × 2
Journal 5Assessment, evaluation, diagnosis, prognosis, intervention with ICF

Long case proforma

Subjective: name, age, sex, occupation demands, hand dominance, address (stairs, distance); chief complaints in the patient's words with duration; HPI: breathlessness (onset, triggers, mMRC or NYHA, orthopnoea, PND), cough and sputum (amount, colour, smell, blood), chest pain, palpitations, fainting, swollen feet, claudication distance, fever, weight loss; past (TB, diabetes, BP, heart, surgery, ventilation); pack-years (cigarettes a day ÷ 20 × years), bidi, tobacco, alcohol, diet, sleep, bladder and bowel; drugs (inhaler technique, beta-blockers, home oxygen); family, support, finances, home, work; the patient's goals.

Objective: HR and rhythm, BP, RR, SpO₂ (air or O₂), temperature, pain; build, BMI, posture, cyanosis, clubbing, pallor, oedema, lines, drains, scars; chest shape, pattern, accessory muscles; trachea, tape expansion at axilla, nipple line, xiphisternum; percussion, auscultation, cough, sputum; pulses incl. feet, capillary refill, pitting grade, postural BP, ABI; shoulder and thoracic range, posture, MRC strength, bed mobility, transfers, gait, balance; 6MWT or shuttle with SpO₂ and Borg, or sit-to-stand; mMRC, Borg, NYHA, CCS, CAT, SGRQ, MLHFQ; X-ray, ABG, PFT, ECG, echo, bloods.

Then: ICF and functional diagnosis → problems → short- and long-term goals → plan with doses and precautions → demonstrate one test (chest expansion, 6MWT set-up, ABI, auscultation, postural BP) and one treatment (ACBT, drainage position, incentive spirometry, monitored Phase I walking).

Presenting (3–5 min)

"Mr X, 55, a bank manager from Pune, day 3 after CABG for triple vessel disease. Main complaints: wound pain, breathlessness on walking. Smoker, 20 pack-years, hypertensive. HR 92 regular, BP 130/84, SpO₂ 95% on air, ↓ sounds at the left base, weak cough, walks 40 m at RPE 12. My functional diagnosis is ...; short-term goals ...; long-term goals ...; plan ... . I will now demonstrate a 6MWT set-up and ACBT."

Viva: why this goal; SpO₂ falls?; stop criteria; progression; what the X-ray or ECG shows; why this dose; drugs and exercise; outcome measures; home programme.

Short case: 8 steps for any skill

  1. Introduce, check identity, explain, consent.
  2. Hand hygiene; privacy, draping; mask in suspected TB.
  3. Vital signs and contraindications for this skill.
  4. Position patient and yourself.
  5. Explain simply → demonstrate → patient does it → correct.
  6. Watch breathing, SpO₂, pain, colour; stop if needed.
  7. State the dose and home programme.
  8. Reassess, thank, document.

Likely skills: ACBT, huffing, supported cough, AD; drainage positions, percussion, vibration; PEP, Flutter, Acapella, MDI + spacer, nebuliser; diaphragmatic and pursed-lip breathing, thoracic expansion, SMI, mobility; NPF, PNF; positions of ease, pacing, relaxation; IMT; 6MWT, shuttle, Harvard step test; ABI, pulses, postural BP, Buerger–Allen; stump and compression bandaging, stockings, burns splints, taping; sternal precautions, log-roll; suction and bagging (manikin); TENS; BLS.

Spots (5 × 2)

  • X-ray: PA or AP; main finding (e.g. left lower lobe collapse: sail sign, raised left diaphragm); two features; physio meaning.
  • ABG: pH → respiratory or metabolic → compensation → oxygenation → name it ("acute type 2 respiratory failure").
  • PFT: FEV1, FVC, ratio → obstructive, restrictive or mixed; severity; reversibility; likely condition.
  • ECG: rate, rhythm, P, QRS width, ST → name → safe to exercise?
  • Device: name, parts, principle, one indication, one precaution.

Journal, affective domain, last two weeks

Journal: each case under assessment, evaluation, ICF and functional diagnosis, prognosis, goals, intervention with doses and progression, outcome measures, SOAP note; examiners read ICF and doses first.

Affective domain: greeting, consent, respectful language, privacy; hand hygiene, infection control; safety checks (vitals, oxygen, lines, drains) and stopping when unwell; explain in Marathi or Hindi and check understanding; honest viva answers ("I don't know, I would find out by..."); leave the patient comfortable.

Last two weeks: present three timed practice long cases aloud (cardiac, respiratory, vascular or surgical); practise each skill on a friend with the 8 steps; spot drills (X-ray, ABG, PFT, ECG, device in 10 min); finish the journal.
