Asked: 3 marks: grades of obesity (S21, inside a cardiac case), define metabolic syndrome (W24). A risk factor in many long cases; 7 marks if asked alone.

Definitions and grades (3m S21)

Obesity: excess body fat that harms health; screened by BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²; waist shows the riskier abdominal fat.

Abdominal obesity (India): waist ≥ 90 cm men, ≥ 80 cm women. A staged Indian definition was proposed in 2025; use the cut-offs above.

Metabolic syndrome (3m W24): any 3 of 5

  • Waist above cut-off (South Asians 90 / 80 cm)
  • Triglycerides ≥ 150 mg/dL
  • HDL below 40 (men) or 50 (women) mg/dL
  • BP ≥ 130/85
  • Fasting glucose ≥ 100 mg/dL

(Or on treatment for any.) Together they raise the risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes.

Causes and effects

Causes: intake above use (calorie-dense food, sugary drinks, fried snacks, big portions; sitting jobs, screens); genes and family habits (South Asians store central fat at lower BMI); poor sleep, stress, steroids, hypothyroidism, Cushing's, PCOS.

Effects: abdominal fat → fatty acids and inflammation → insulin resistance, high BP, abnormal lipids → diabetes and heart disease; heavy chest wall and abdomen → ↓ lung volumes (worse lying flat), ↑ work of breathing, sleep apnoea, obesity hypoventilation; knee, hip, back load → osteoarthritis; low fitness, heat intolerance, stigma → less activity.

Features: high BMI and waist, exertional breathlessness, snoring, daytime sleepiness, joint pain, acanthosis nigricans, stretch marks, low mood. Complications: diabetes, hypertension, CHD, stroke, fatty liver, sleep apnoea, post-op chest problems and sternal wound problems, OA, DVT, some cancers.

Tests: BMI, waist, waist-to-hip ratio, skinfolds or bioimpedance; glucose, HbA1c, lipids, liver, thyroid; BP; sleep study; 6MWT. Medical: lifestyle first (5–10% weight loss already improves BP, sugar, lipids); GLP-1 agonists; bariatric surgery for severe obesity.

Assessment and goals

Weight, height, BMI, waist; BP with a large cuff, resting HR; activity, sitting time, diet, sleep, motivation and barriers; joint pain, range, posture, balance; 6MWT with Borg; sit-to-stand. Outcomes: weight, waist, 6MWT, sit-to-stand, step count, QoL.

In an ICF (S21 businessman after CABG, BMI 32): class I obesity (obese by Indian cut-off), insulin resistance, low fitness, heavy chest wall (body function); diet, sitting job, stress (personal).

Goals: short-term (4–6 weeks): walk 30 min most days, break sitting every 30 min, activity and food diary. Long-term (6 months): lose 5–10% weight, smaller waist, > 250 min a week + resistance twice, better 6MWT.

  • Low-impact aerobic first: brisk walking, cycling, swimming, water exercise (spare knees).
  • Moderate intensity (talk test, RPE 12–13); start with 10-min bouts, add 5 min a week.
  • Resistance 2–3 days a week: keeps muscle while losing fat, better insulin sensitivity.
  • Lifestyle activity (stairs, walking to shops); dietitian; behaviour change (goals, self-monitoring, family).

Precautions and education

Screen for heart disease, diabetes and sleep apnoea before vigorous work; low-impact for painful joints, good shoes; cooler hours, water; large cuff; avoid lying flat long; breathable clothes (chafing). Education: lose ~0.5 kg a week; fewer sugary drinks, sweets, fried snacks, maida; more vegetables, pulses, whole grains; smaller portions; sleep 7–8 h; check weight and waist monthly. Fitness helps even if weight changes little.
