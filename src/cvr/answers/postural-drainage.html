Any six contraindications for postural drainage

Postural drainage positions the patient so that the affected segment's bronchus points down towards the trachea and gravity helps drain secretions. Most contraindications apply to the head-down tilt:

  1. Recent or active haemoptysis.
  2. Raised intracranial pressure, recent head injury or neurosurgery.
  3. Unstable heart: uncontrolled hypertension, arrhythmia, recent MI, pulmonary oedema, heart failure.
  4. Severe breathlessness or bronchospasm.
  5. Reflux, hiatus hernia, recent oesophageal surgery; within an hour of a meal.
  6. Aortic aneurysm, recent eye surgery.
  7. Gross obesity, abdominal distension, late pregnancy.
  8. Untreated pneumothorax, large effusion or empyema, rib fractures, painful recent surgery.

When tilting is contraindicated, use modified positions (side-lying or prone without head-down). Neonates: no head-down tilt.
