Define metabolic syndrome

Metabolic syndrome is a cluster of risk factors (central obesity, raised BP, raised blood sugar, high triglycerides, low HDL) that together raise the risk of heart disease and type 2 diabetes.

Diagnosis (harmonised definition): any 3 of 5

  1. Waist above the population cut-off: 90 cm or more in South Asian men, 80 cm or more in women.
  2. Triglycerides 150 mg/dL or more (or on treatment).
  3. HDL below 40 mg/dL in men, below 50 mg/dL in women (or on treatment).
  4. BP 130/85 mmHg or more (or on treatment).
  5. Fasting glucose 100 mg/dL or more (or on treatment).

Core problem: abdominal fat causing insulin resistance. Treatment: weight loss of 5–10%, at least 150–250 min a week of activity, diet.
