?How to use these notes

  1. Read a page once, slowly. Open every "Why?" button.
  2. Close it and recall. Write the keywords and draw the diagrams on paper from memory, then compare.
  3. Test yourself with the questions at the end of the page. Answer before you open the answer.
  4. Come back on day 1, day 7 and day 21, and tick the boxes at the top of the page.

Every page starts with "Home" at the top, so you can always come back here.
